What the Agent Sees (Observation Space)

Every 5 seconds, the agent reads these 10 numbers from SUMO's TraCI API:

Queue Lengths (halting vehicles per edge)

N_to_C (Northbound) obs[0]
S_to_C (Southbound) obs[1]
E_to_C (Eastbound) obs[2]
W_to_C (Westbound) obs[3]

Mean Speeds (m/s per edge)

N_to_C (Northbound) obs[4]
S_to_C (Southbound) obs[5]
E_to_C (Eastbound) obs[6]
W_to_C (Westbound) obs[7]

Signal State

Current Phase obs[8] = 0 (NS) or 1 (EW)
Time in Phase / 60 obs[9] = 0.0 to 1.0

What DQN Does NOT See

Arrival rate forecasts ❌
LSTM turn predictions ❌
Per-vehicle trajectories ❌
Through-traffic ratios ❌

Why Our Adaptive Method Wins

🔮
Richer Signals
LSTM predicts which vehicles will go straight vs turn. Arrival rate forecasts future demand. DQN only sees current queues and speeds.
🎛️
Per-Scenario Tuning
Each scenario gets its own Optuna-optimized weights (e.g., v2 uses C=53s, v4 uses C=30s). DQN uses one fixed policy for all.
📋
Full Explainability
Every decision has a complete equation breakdown: queue terms, arrival terms, through-bonus, green allocation. DQN is a black box.
